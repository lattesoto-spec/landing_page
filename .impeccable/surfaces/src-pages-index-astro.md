# Surface brief: CareMin landing page

Scope: `src/pages/index.astro` with shared header, footer, and global styles. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: aged-care executives, operations, facility and compliance managers. Action: arrange a walkthrough. Proof: real product screens, Monash and Fastrack backing, primary-source links. Constraints: copy unchanged, no em dashes, no decorative underlines, self-hosted fonts only (CSP).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A well-signed building. The page orients like a wayfinding program: one clear primary sign per section, colour-coded zones, a visible route from source file to output. It refuses the white split hero with logo row and identical icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep signage ink (#0E2B35) and mineral white panels, CareMin aqua as a zone field and route colour, a muted eucalyptus as the secondary zone. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next only, large and heavy for signs, regular for text. Plaques: rounded panels with a satin edge, used for product screens and the form. Route: a continuous line with station markers.

STORY: Visitors learn CareMin works from the exports they already have, with no migration, then see the route Import, Classify, Monitor, Export, then trust the traceability and data handling, then arrange a walkthrough.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink field. Left: the headline as a primary sign at around 4.5rem, summary, aqua walkthrough button and sign in. Right: the product screen as a mounted plaque. Along the bottom: backing statement and logos on a light strip.

FORM: Wayfinding Program, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:7c2aa6ee8191eabc

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The route strip. On load the route line draws from Import to Export once. Station markers on the lineage panel highlight on hover and focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
