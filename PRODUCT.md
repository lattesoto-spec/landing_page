# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Australian residential aged-care provider teams: executives and operations leaders overseeing
multiple homes, facility managers, Directors of Nursing, compliance managers, and finance teams.
They evaluate CareMin while preparing care-minute, 24/7 RN, QFR, and Care Minutes Performance
Statement reporting.

## Product Purpose

CareMin turns the staffing, roster, and occupied bed-day files a provider already exports into
care-minute results, RN coverage findings, and traceable evidence. Success on the marketing site is
a visitor arranging a walkthrough. Sign in is secondary, for existing customers.

## Positioning

System-agnostic: providers keep their existing rostering and payroll systems. No migration is
needed. CareMin works from existing exports and links every result to its source file, row,
eligibility decision, and calculation version.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Imports of common structured exports; reusable role classifications.
- Daily and quarter-to-date care-minute monitoring against each home's own targets.
- RN coverage timeline, evidence lineage, CSV/Excel/PDF outputs, planning scenarios.
- Multiple facilities per provider.
- Australian data hosting; Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) handling.
- The contact form posts to FormSubmit; CSP allows only self-hosted fonts and images.

## Brand Commitments

- Existing page copy is approved by the owner and must not change during visual work.
- No em dashes in written copy.
- Avoid underlines as decoration across the page.

## Evidence on Hand

- Backing: Monash University and Fastrack (its startup accelerator). Logos in `src/assets/partners/`.
- Real product screens with demonstration data in `src/assets/product/`.
- No customer logos, testimonials, or pilot metrics exist. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with what CareMin provides and that it fits existing systems without migration.
2. Every claim stays concrete and traceable, matching the product's own promise.
3. Conversion to a walkthrough outranks visual flourish.
